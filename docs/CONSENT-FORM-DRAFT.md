# Speaker consent and release form (DRAFT, NOT IN USE, NOT LEGAL ADVICE)

This wording has not been reviewed by a lawyer and does not yet exist in Somali. Do not use it to collect recordings. It is published so people can criticise it.

The speaker must be able to read and understand it in Somali.

> I, [name], confirm that:
> 1. I am recording my own voice and I am 18 or older (or my guardian signs below).
> 2. I understand my recordings will be published openly under [LICENCE NAME] and that anyone, anywhere, may copy, use and train computer programs on them, including programs that generate speech that sounds like me. I understand this includes commercial use if the licence allows it.
> 3. I understand that once published I cannot fully take recordings back from copies others have made, but I may ask the project to remove my recordings from future releases by opening an issue or using the contact the project then lists, and the project will do so within [TIME] where it can.
> 4. I will not read text I do not have the right to share, and I have not been paid or pressured to agree. [If payment is made, state the amount and the date here.]
> 5. I choose to be identified in the dataset as: [ ] a numeric ID only [ ] my name [ ] other: ____
> 6. Optional: I agree to share my age band, gender and region or dialect, to be published as [ ] yes [ ] no.
> Signature / date / (guardian signature if under 18)

Storage: signed forms are kept privately by the project maintainer, never in the public repository. The public dataset carries only an anonymous consent ID.

Open Somali Speech: listen

Day one. Below is everything you can hear today, and exactly what it is.

Human recordings: none yet. No Somali speaker has recorded for this project, and we will not collect any until the consent process is reviewed and available in Somali. These clips are machine-made, shown so you can judge the starting point and why this project is needed.

Synthetic demo clips

Made with Meta's MMS-TTS Somali model (licence CC-BY-NC 4.0, non-commercial) using our own scripts. Each clip is the best of 8 tries, picked by a speech recogniser, which is our reliability idea in action. The phrases are everyday Somali we chose ourselves, not text from any book.

How to read these honestly

  • These are not our model and not a human voice. They come from a third-party model we can only use non-commercially. Do not use them to train an open model.
  • The recogniser that scored them is from the same family as the voice model, so it can favour that model's pronunciation. A Somali listener is the real judge. If you speak Somali, tell us what you hear: open an issue.
  • The 16 kHz output is thin compared with studio speech.
  • Full per-try numbers are in report.json.

What comes next

Review of the hard-word list by Somali speakers, a reviewed consent process, then recordings from consenting speakers under a clear open licence. Read the README and CONTRIBUTING.

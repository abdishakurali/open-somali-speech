# Risks (one page, 2026-11-09)

| # | Risk | What we know | Mitigation | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Copyright of the Somali translation.** Releasing text or audio built on it could infringe | archive.org item has no licence field; the book names the King Fahd Complex as publisher and the translator is marked deceased; no copyright notice found in the part read; our own docs say "rights unverified" (see README, section Not included) | Do not publish it. Ask the Complex in writing, or commission/choose a translation with a clear licence. Launch with original contributor text first | OPEN. The single biggest blocker |
| 2 | **Speaker consent and voice-clone misuse.** Openly licensed voice data can be used to clone a real person's voice for fraud or false religious statements | Plan Phase 1 wants one speaker with a signed release; an open licence cannot restrict misuse by itself | Signed Somali-language release naming the open licence and cloning risk; optional gated access for voice data; speaker can choose anonymity; label synthetic audio; no cloning of a named reciter without their written yes. Lawyer to review the form | OPEN, form is a draft |
| 3 | **MMS CC-BY-NC contamination.** Anything trained or fine-tuned from `facebook/mms-tts-som` stays CC-BY-NC (model card, verified 2026-11-09); OmniVoice weights are also CC-BY-NC (earlier check) | Using their audio as training data or as labels for an "open" model would taint it | Do not use MMS/OmniVoice output as data. Use them only as private benchmarks. Start any model from a permissive base and verify its licence on the repo. Use an independent ASR (the MMS ASR in our check favours MMS pronunciation) | Known; keep a written rule |
| 4 | **Contributor privacy.** Voices, names, region and age identify people; forms hold signatures | Not yet collected | Anonymous speaker IDs; forms stored privately, never in the repo; demographics opt-in; collect the minimum; state retention and a takedown contact; strip audio metadata; no contributor email in public files | Design needed before first contribution |
| 5 | **Moderation of contributions.** Sentences may carry hate, clan abuse, private data, scraped copyrighted text, or false claims about scripture | No process yet | Two-reviewer rule (CONTRIBUTING.md section C), reject on unclear source, report channel, removal log, reviewers named by ID not exposed publicly | Draft |
| 6 | **Religious text accuracy.** Mispronounced or mistranslated scripture does harm | Our own AI drafts are labelled unreviewed; MMS mispronounced "mahad" in 1 of 4 runs | Keep scripture out of the first release; any scripture audio needs native review of each clip | Scoped out of v1 |
| 7 | **Overclaiming.** "Open" while rights are unclear; benchmark claims from a biased checker | See above | Say "no data released yet"; state limits in README and DATA-CARD | Done in drafts |

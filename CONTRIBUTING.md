# Contributing

Thank you for helping. Please read this page; it protects you and the project.

## What we accept now
| Contribution | Accepted now? | How |
|---|---|---|
| Review and extend `hard-words.txt` | Yes | Open an issue or pull request. Words only. Do not copy sentences from books, websites or translations |
| Code: evaluation scripts, an independent speech recognizer check, recording and review tools | Yes | Pull request, Apache-2.0 |
| Rights checks, legal review of the consent wording, ideas | Yes | Open an issue |
| Somali **text** for a corpus | Not yet | Our review process is not set up. Contribute to Qor Af-Soomaali (https://qor.unkad.com), an existing community corpus, for now |
| **Voice recordings** | **No, not yet** | We will not collect any voice until the consent process is reviewed by a lawyer and available in Somali. Please do not send recordings to anyone listed here |

## Ground rules (they will apply to all future text and audio)
1. **Only contribute what you have the right to share.** Do not copy text from books, websites, news, WhatsApp groups or religious publications unless it is clearly free to reuse or you hold the rights. "I found it online" is not permission.
2. **Never contribute someone else's voice.** Only record yourself.
3. **Do not include private information** (phone numbers, addresses, names of private people, health or family details).
4. You must be 18 or older, or have a parent or guardian sign.
5. Anything accepted into a public release cannot be fully recalled from copies others have already downloaded. We will publish a removal route, but it cannot reach copies already made.
6. **Scripture:** do not submit translations of the Quran or hadith unless you are the translator or can show a written licence.

## Review (planned for every contribution)
Nothing will be released without review by two people: a Somali reader for text (spelling, original or licensed, no private or harmful content), and a Somali listener for audio (matches the transcript exactly, a signed form is on file, clean and unedited). Rejected items are deleted.

## Consent
The draft wording is in `docs/CONSENT-FORM-DRAFT.md`. It is not in use. Signed forms will be kept privately and never in this repository.

## Conduct
Be respectful. No hate, harassment, clan abuse, or content that targets private people. Report problems by opening an issue (do not post private details in it).
